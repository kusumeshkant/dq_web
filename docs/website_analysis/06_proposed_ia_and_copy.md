## 4. Section copy (drafts)

**Problem**
- H2: The rack sells it. The billing queue loses it.
- Body: On weekends and sale days, shoppers with a full basket see the line and leave. Your staff are stuck billing instead of selling on the floor.
- (No statistics unless you source them. C-47.)

**How it works**
- H2: Shop. Scan. Pay. Walk out.
- Steps:
  1. Scan the store QR at the entrance.
  2. Scan product tags as you shop.
  3. Pay in the app with UPI or card.
  4. Show your exit pass. Staff check it in a second.

**Keep your till**
- H2: Keep your counter. Add a faster lane.
- Body: DQ isn't a new POS. Shoppers who want the counter still use it. Everyone else checks out on their phone. No scanners, no kiosks, nothing to install in-store.

**Owner control** (the differentiator)
- H2: Discounts your staff can't misuse.
- Bullets:
  - Staff ask for discount authority. You decide the limit, and you can approve less than they asked for.
  - Every discount is logged with the staff name, bill and amount, and the log can't be edited.
  - Staff can never change a price or stock count.
- Hindi H2: **डिस्काउंट पर पूरा कंट्रोल — आपके हाथ में।**

**See your store**
- H2: Know your busiest hour, and who's giving discounts.
- Bullets: Peak hours and days · Baskets started but not paid · Discount depth by staff · Top products and week-on-week sales.
- Label: *Sample store data*.

**What you need**
- H2: What you need to start
- Checklist:
  - ✔ Products with barcodes or tags
  - ✔ Your product list in Excel (barcode, name, price, stock)
  - ✔ One staff phone at the exit
  - ✔ Store internet
  - ✘ No new hardware
- Don't add "your Razorpay account" until the settlement model is decided (`07` Q3).

**Setup**
- H2: Live in 3 screens.
- Steps: Store details → Upload your Excel → Get your store code and QR poster.
- Caption: Under 5 minutes once your product list is ready. We'll help you prepare it.

**Objections FAQ (rewritten answers)**
- *Do shoppers need to download an app?* → answer depends on `07` Q1. Never answer "no download" while web pay is off.
- *What about theft?* → Every shopper shows an exit pass linked to a paid order. Staff check it in one tap.
- *Do I need hardware?* → No. Staff use their own phone.
- *What if the internet drops?* → DQ needs internet. Shoppers can use your counter as usual.
- *Will it replace my POS?* → No. It runs alongside it.
- *How long does setup take?* → Under 5 minutes in the app. Preparing your product list is the main job, and we help with it.

**Final CTA**
- H2: Be one of our first stores.
- Body: We're onboarding a small group of founding stores. You get DQ free during the pilot, plus founder-led setup. In return, we ask for your honest feedback.
- CTA: **Apply as a founding store** · WhatsApp link.

## 5. Honest pricing wording (`/pricing`)

>
> CTA: **Apply as a founding store**
